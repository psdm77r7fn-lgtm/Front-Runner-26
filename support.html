Support

Need help or found a problem? Email rrapps@icloud.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened just before the problem.

How to race

Set your pace with the slider or buttons. Faster paces drain stamina, and running behind another athlete saves energy. Tap Kick once for a sprint finish, but go too early and you will fade before the line.

Common questions

Where are my results saved?
On your device. They stay when you close the app, but deleting the app removes them.
Can I play offline?
Yes. The game works fully without an internet connection.
There is no sound.
Check the sound button in the race and the switch in Settings, and make sure your iPhone is not on silent mode.
How do I change my name or country?
Open Settings and tap Your name and country. Your name and flag appear on the results board, the lap updates and the medal ceremony. In the race your runner is always labelled YOU.
How do I turn off the vibration?
Open Settings and switch Haptics off.
What do WR, CR, PB and SB mean?
On the results board: WR is a world record, CR a championship record, PB a personal best and SB a season best. An "=" in front, such as =PB, means the record was equalled to the hundredth of a second.
What are the Classic races?
Seven famous Grand Games finals, recreated with their historical fields, records, conditions and race shapes. You take the winner’s place. Every classic is run at Elite difficulty, and you can earn up to three stars in each.
How do I save or share a podium photo?
When you win a medal, tap Podium photo during the ceremony. Press and hold the photo to save it, or tap Share. The ceremony waits until you tap Continue.
Where is the Medal cabinet?
Open Records and tap Medal cabinet. It keeps a card for every podium and lets you choose your podium celebration. New celebrations unlock as you win more medals.
How does a Career season work?
Each season starts with a solo time trial that sets the opening ranking. At each meeting the top eight in the standings race the A race and everyone else the B race, which scores fewer points. Grand Prix meetings invite only the top eight. Reach the Championship with the entry standard or a top-eight ranking. In Grand Games seasons (8, 12 and 15) you must race at least three meetings and finish in the top three at the Grand Games Trials.
What are Form, Fitness and Fatigue?
Fitness is built by speed and endurance training. Fatigue comes from racing and hard training, and rest clears it. Form is how ready you are to race: fitness minus fatigue, plus sharpness. In the last three training blocks before the big race, choosing Rest or Race sharpness builds your peak. Hard training resets it, and missing meetings leaves you with race rust.
Which graphics setting should I use?
New players start on High. If the game ever runs slowly, it switches to Medium once and tells you. You can choose Low, Medium or High yourself in Settings, and the game will not change your choice.
Is the game free?
Yes. There are no adverts and no in-app purchases.
