Support

Need help or found a problem? Email rrapps@icloud.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened just before the problem.

How to race

Set your pace with the slider or buttons. Faster paces drain stamina, and running behind another athlete saves energy. Tap Kick once for a sprint finish, but go too early and you will fade before the line.

Common questions

Where are my results saved?
On your device. They stay when you close the app, but deleting the app removes them.
Can I play offline?
Yes. The game works fully without an internet connection.
There is no sound.
Check the sound button in the race and the switch in Settings, and make sure your iPhone is not on silent mode.
Is the game free?
Yes. There are no adverts and no in-app purchases.
