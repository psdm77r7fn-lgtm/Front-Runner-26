Support

Need help or found a problem? Email rrapps@icloud.com. It helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened just before the problem.

How to race

Set your pace with the slider or buttons. Faster paces drain stamina, and running behind another athlete saves energy. Tap Kick once for a sprint finish, but go too early and you will fade before the line.

Common questions

Where are my results saved?
On your device. They stay when you close the app, but deleting the app removes them.
Can I play offline?
Yes. The game works fully without an internet connection.
There is no sound.
Check the sound button in the race and the switch in Settings, and make sure your iPhone is not on silent mode. The music has its own music button and its own on/off switch in the Music tab, so the game sounds and the music can be turned on and off separately.
How do I change my name or country?
Open Settings and tap Your name and country. Your name and flag appear on the results board, the lap updates and the medal ceremony. In the race your runner is always labelled YOU.
How do I turn off the vibration?
Open Settings and switch Haptics off.
What do WR, CR, PB and SB mean?
On the results board: WR is a world record, CR a championship record, PB a personal best and SB a season best. An "=" in front, such as =PB, means the record was equalled to the hundredth of a second.
What are the Classic races?
Eight famous Grand Games finals, recreated with their historical fields, records, conditions and race shapes. You take the winner’s place. Every classic is run at Elite difficulty, and you can earn up to three stars in each.
How do I save or share a podium photo?
When you win a medal, tap Podium photo during the ceremony. Press and hold the photo to save it, or tap Share. The ceremony waits until you tap Continue.
Where is the Medal cabinet?
Open Records and tap Medal cabinet. It keeps a card for every podium and lets you choose your podium celebration. New celebrations unlock as you win more medals.
How does a Career season work?
Each season starts with a solo time trial that sets the opening ranking. At each meeting the top eight in the standings race the A race and everyone else the B race, which scores fewer points. Grand Prix meetings invite only the top eight. In seasons 1 to 3, finish in the top three at the trials to reach the championship. From season 4, reach the Championship with the entry standard or a top-eight ranking. In Grand Games seasons (8, 12 and 15) you must race at least three meetings and finish in the top three at the Grand Games Trials.
What are Form, Fitness and Fatigue?
Fitness is built by speed and endurance training. Fatigue comes from racing and hard training, and rest clears it. Form is how ready you are to race: fitness minus fatigue, plus sharpness. In the last three training blocks before the big race, choosing Rest or Race sharpness builds your peak. Hard training resets it, and missing meetings leaves you with race rust.
Which graphics setting should I use?
New players start on High. If the game ever runs slowly, it switches to Medium once and tells you. You can choose Low, Medium or High yourself in Settings, and the game will not change your choice.
How do the Grand Games work?
Choose the 1500m (three races over five days) or the double (800m and 1500m, six races over seven days). Heats lead to semi-finals and a final; the top places go through automatically, then the fastest losers. Rest days help you and your rivals recover. The Grand Games are always run at Elite difficulty.
What is the Nerves meter?
At the Grand Games you can get nervous, which makes your pace wander a little and costs some energy. Nerves are highest in the final, fade as the race settles and come back on the last lap. Experience helps: they ease with every Games you run.
How do I start a new Championship or Grand Games?
Tap the mode to open Your saves. Each mode has three save files: tap one to continue it, choose New on an empty one, or tap Delete twice to clear one.
Where are the Challenges?
Tap Challenges on the main menu. Each challenge earns a bronze, silver or gold star when you complete it. Grand Games stars count in Grand Games mode or in an Elite career.
What does the * next to a time in Records mean?
It marks a time set in Career mode.
What are the Town Classics?
Road races through three towns: the Ashbury 1500, the Fenwick Mile and the Jaffna 2K. Race Day is the town’s big race against an elite field; you can also train round the town with your club-mate Ollie. Every course has a personal best and a course record, held at first by a local runner.
What is the mid-season break in Career?
After meeting 4 the season stops for a week. Choose Ashbury, Chipping Fenwick or Jaffna and have one outing there: race the town's Race Day against your season rivals, or train with your partner. A win brings a boost back with no extra fatigue: Race Day gives speed and endurance, the sprint gives speed and the long run gives endurance. The result stands, so choose carefully. Or stay home and train as normal.
What happens when I win a town race?
The action slows down as you break the tape, then a banner shows your time and whether it is a new PB or course record. Tap to go straight to the results.
How do I choose the music?
Open Music on the menu (Front Runner Radio). It is a full player: play, pause, skip, shuffle, repeat, a music volume and a sleep timer. Tick the songs you want in the game; the ticked songs play in the menu and in races. Music on/off is in Settings.
Can the music keep playing when I close the app?
Yes. The music carries on when you leave the app or lock your phone, and you can pause, skip back or forward and move through the song from the lock screen. To stop it, tap Keeps playing when you leave in the Music player so it says Stops when you leave.
How do the Game Center leaderboards work?
If you are signed in to Game Center (Settings > Game Center on your iPhone), your time goes to the leaderboard whenever you finish a full 1500m: a quick race, a Championship, the Grand Games or a Classic race. There are leaderboards for men and women. Career races run on their own clock, so they don’t count, and neither do races joined part-way round. To see them, open Records and tap Game Center leaderboard. Game Center keeps your best time, and the first time you sign in, your existing personal bests are sent too.
Why does the leaderboard show hours, such as 0:03:24.68?
That is how Apple’s Game Center shows times. 0:03:24.68 is 3:24.68.
Where has Season mode gone?
Season mode has been retired in version 1.5. Career has the same eight-meeting seasons, with rivals, training, the mid-season break and championships around them. Saved seasons stay on your device.
Who made the music?
The songs are original tracks by rukrav, made for Front Runner 26.
How do I save a record photo?
When a record falls, the record cut scene plays. If it is your record, tap Share there, or tap Record photo on the results screen after a world record.
Is the game free?
Yes. There are no adverts and no in-app purchases.
