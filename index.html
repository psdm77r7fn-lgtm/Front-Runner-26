Front Runner 26 is a 3D athletics game about how races are really won. Choose your race plan, manage your stamina, draft behind rivals and decide when to make your move. Race a single final, chase a championship, relive famous Grand Games finals, or take one athlete through a fifteen-season career.

Free on iPhone. Version 1.2.

Races and modes

  • Quick raceOne 1500m final: three and three-quarter laps, one kick
  • ChampionshipHeats, semi-final and final
  • SeasonEight meetings and a title race
  • CareerFifteen seasons, from the school trials to three Grand Games
  • Classic racesSeven famous Grand Games finals, recreated

Coming in future updates: the 5000m, 800m, steeplechase, relays and field events.

No ads, no accounts

The game is free to play, has no adverts or in-app purchases, and works offline. Your results stay on your device.

New in version 1.2

  • Classic racesRelive seven famous finals from 1960 to 2021, with their historical fields, records, times of day and conditions. Rivals race the way the real finals unfolded
  • Medal ceremonyA podium in the infield with flags rising and medals for all three. Take a podium photo to share the moment
  • Medal cabinetEvery podium you earn, kept as a card, plus podium celebrations to unlock
  • A new careerA season time trial, A and B races by ranking, Grand Prix invitations, named rivals, the Athletes’ Choice award and three Grand Games
  • Form, fitness and fatigueTrain, rest and time your peak for the season’s big race
  • A new lookRedesigned menus, a crowd that fills the stands, numbered bibs, a photo-finish camera and national flags on the start line

Also: automatic graphics that start on High and adjust for your iPhone, fairer points and many smaller fixes.
