Pace yourself, sit in the pack and time your kick.

Front Runner 26 is a 3D athletics game about how races are really won. Choose your race plan, manage your stamina, draft behind rivals and decide when to make your move. Race a single final, chase a championship, or take one athlete through a ten-season career.

Races and modes

  • 1500mThree and three-quarter laps, one kick
  • 5000mTwelve and a half laps of tactics
  • ChampionshipHeats, semi-final and final
  • SeasonEight meetings and a title race
  • CareerTen seasons, rivals who age and retire

No ads, no accounts

The game is free to play, has no adverts or in-app purchases, and works offline. Your results stay on your device.
