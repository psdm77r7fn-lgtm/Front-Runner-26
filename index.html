Front Runner 26 is a 3D athletics game about how races are really won. Choose your race plan, manage your stamina, draft behind rivals and decide when to make your move. Race a single final, chase a championship, go for gold at the Grand Games, relive famous finals, or take one athlete through a fifteen-season career.

Free on iPhone. Version 1.3.

Races and modes

  • Quick raceOne 1500m final: three and three-quarter laps, one kick
  • ChampionshipHeats, semi-final and final
  • SeasonEight meetings and a title race
  • CareerFifteen seasons, from the school trials to three Grand Games
  • Grand GamesFive days of heats, semi-finals and a final: the 1500m, or the 800m and 1500m double
  • Classic racesEight famous Grand Games finals, recreated
  • ChallengesEighteen bronze, silver and gold stars to earn

Coming in future updates: the 5000m, steeplechase, relays and field events.

No ads, no accounts

The game is free to play, has no adverts or in-app purchases, and works offline. Your results stay on your device.

New in version 1.3

  • The Grand GamesA host city, five days of racing with a forecast for each day, full heats and semi-finals with fastest losers, a walk-out for the final and a medal ceremony
  • Big-stage storylinesFace the favourite, the defending champion and the home hero, and keep your nerves in check as the pressure builds
  • The 800mTwo laps from a staggered lane start, raced at the Grand Games as part of the 800m and 1500m double
  • ChallengesEighteen bronze, silver and gold stars, from your first championship win to Elite gold
  • A new classic1996: Chaos at the bell
  • Named rivalsEvery athlete races under their own name and flag. Rivals tire after hard races and recover over time

Also: three save files each for Season, Championship and Grand Games, redesigned Records with your top 10, longer names and many smaller fixes.
