Front Runner26

Pace yourself, sit in the pack and time your kick.

HomeSupportPrivacy

Front Runner 26 is a 3D athletics game about how races are really won. Choose your race plan, manage your stamina, draft behind rivals and decide when to make your move. Race a single final, chase a championship, go for gold at the Grand Games, relive famous finals, run the Town Classics through three towns, or take one athlete through a fifteen-season career. Then put your fastest 1500m on the Game Center leaderboards.

Free on iPhone. Version 1.5.

Races and modes

  • Quick raceOne 1500m final: three and three-quarter laps, one kick
  • ChampionshipHeats, semi-final and final
  • CareerFifteen seasons, from the school trials to three Grand Games
  • Grand GamesFive days of heats, semi-finals and a final: the 1500m, or the 800m and 1500m double
  • Classic racesEight famous Grand Games finals, recreated
  • ChallengesEighteen bronze, silver and gold stars to earn
  • Town ClassicsRoad races through Ashbury, Chipping Fenwick and Jaffna, on Race Day or training with Ollie
  • MusicFront Runner Radio: the twelve-song soundtrack in a full music player

Coming in future updates: the 5000m, steeplechase, relays and more field events.

No ads, no accounts

The game is free to play, has no adverts or in-app purchases, and works offline. Your results stay on your device. If you use Game Center, your fastest 1500m times also go to the Game Center leaderboards.
