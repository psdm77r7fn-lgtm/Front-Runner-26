Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Front Runner 26 (“the app”) handles your information. In short: the app does not collect any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use any third-party services that collect data.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves your game data on your device only. This includes race results, personal bests, records, season and career progress, and your settings. This data is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that uses data, such as online leaderboards, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: rrapps@icloud.com
