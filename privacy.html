Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Front Runner 26 (“the app”) handles your information. In short: the app does not collect any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information. When you first open the app it asks for a name and country to show on your results. These are what you choose to type in, they are stored only on your device, and they are never sent to us or anyone else. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, does not use any third-party services that collect data, and does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves your game data on your device only. This includes race results, personal bests, records, season and career progress, classic race stars, your medal cabinet and chosen podium celebration, your settings, and the name and country you entered. This data is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Podium photos

The app can create a podium photo when you win a medal. The photo is made on your device and is not uploaded anywhere. It is only saved or shared if you choose to, using your iPhone’s own save or share options.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that uses data, such as online leaderboards, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: rrapps@icloud.com
